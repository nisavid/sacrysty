# Real FIDO worker qualification: requirement and evidence inventory

This inventory prepares the next public evidence handoff for [Qualify the FIDO custody adapter](https://github.com/nisavid/sacrysty/issues/16). It is anchored to Sacrysty commit [`04f081ebc37f9d3574d7af9e0bb246d645a39cf4`](https://github.com/nisavid/sacrysty/commit/04f081ebc37f9d3574d7af9e0bb246d645a39cf4) and tree `9b57af85a777eaee57fa47e940987e471d6381c0`. It records requirements and missing producer inputs; it contains no real-worker execution or qualification result. The existing issue checklist remains open.

The controlling decision is [Settle the real FIDO worker's verification and evidence contract](https://github.com/nisavid/sacrysty/issues/29#issuecomment-5916221533). [FIDO worker interfaces: bounded research record](../../docs/research/fido-worker-interfaces.md) and [Generic FIDO plugin upstream baseline](../../docs/research/fido-upstream-baseline.md) supply source and research observations, not selected runtime artifacts. The implementation owner must resolve their limits in reviewed source, an ADR, threat-model review, conformance, and compatibility evidence before this inventory can become an execution plan. [Establish Sacrysty's immutable public genesis](https://github.com/nisavid/sacrysty/issues/3) owns that source handoff; the qualification owner does not choose its protocol or custody semantics.

## Evidence already held, with its original limits

The [merged synthetic CI receipt](https://github.com/nisavid/sacrysty/issues/16#issuecomment-5897768921) identifies squash commit `3cb793d2fee306df9b4960d5f9460b19d84eab85`, whose tree matched reviewed and tested candidate `1b63ff9703d25f3cafc5fa37fafa3c46c511cf50`; its frozen checked source is `81896a0c9f0b5af4f8c7e69e7d48da34e8e91318`. It reports all 12 checks passing before merge and resolved review threads. The maintained [synthetic qualification README](README.md) identifies the seven direct runtime source paths and explains that the workflow and runner, rather than the [preserved September 16 preparation](preparation-2026-09-16.md), select the current synthetic source. The emitted result's workflow, executable, runtime, platform and image facts belong to that run; this inventory does not copy them into a new tuple or claim that its synthetic observations executed a real worker.

The [interface research](../../docs/research/fido-worker-interfaces.md) froze age v1.3.2 and Typage v0.3.1 source, made disposable Linux/amd64 x/crypto v0.56.0 builds, and observed a fake plugin on the **recipient-v1 encryption** path. Its no-terminal secret request received `fail`; its cancellation fixture recorded fake-plugin EOF before final cleanup. Those observations do not cover identity-v1 decryption, the Typage plugin, a FIDO library in operation, an authenticator, or a real worker. The [upstream baseline](../../docs/research/fido-upstream-baseline.md) independently inventories Typage source at `38b8b10cb22409de0eaa8a617a01f16dc2e3f9f4`; the upstream TypeScript CI and release jobs do not supply Go plugin binary provenance. Neither research candidate is an admitted executable selection.

## Dependent execution and joins

Before dependent source validation, load the implementation revision's maintained [Validate a genesis integration candidate](../../docs/agents/genesis-validation.md) procedure. Record the revision of that procedure and verify its producer result: the candidate's source inventory against the retained bundle, the value-free conformance and complete source-validation receipt, and the published commit/tree reconciliation where a merged candidate is consumed. The receipt must identify the candidate commit and tree and show the procedure's per-step passes on that same source. A passing source-validation receipt proves only its exercised layer; it cannot stand in for artifact admission, platform qualification, security review, or hardware observation. The qualification runner's [README](README.md) remains the entrypoint for its narrower synthetic source selection.

Only after the implementation owner supplies a reviewed immutable worker, selected executable/dependency closure, public profile, fixtures, and the procedure result can independent Linux and GitHub-hosted macOS build, dependency, fixture, and simulated checks run in disposable environments. They may run concurrently; each produces its own platform tuple. Join them only after verifying each tuple's source, worker, dependency, profile, fixture, workflow, runtime, architecture, and runner-image bindings and obtaining current independent review for the final revisions. A changed input or evidence dependency makes the affected pass stale and sends it back to the owning producer. The final qualification record must state support, failure, recovery, retirement, and unqualified limits from the applicable observed results before any downstream public handoff to [Adopt the qualified FIDO custody adapter for release signing](https://github.com/nisavid/dotfiles/issues/223).

This preparation leaves the issue's three acceptance items open. It neither runs a real worker nor establishes a pass for plugin, hardware, provider, custody, release, adoption, or production use.
